About me

As a kid I loved to play video games, but when I grew a bit older I also wanted to know how the games worked and how they are made. This got me started with making some 2D games.


I love to be hands on when learning something new. Learning by doing works the best for me. I also learned the value of breaking up a large project or feature in smaller parts that are actually manageable. This helped me create complex features without it becoming overwhelming. When I am not behind my keyboard I hope to be on the slopes with a snowboard as much as possible, which is a challenge in a country without any mountains...
