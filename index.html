About me

I grew up loving video games, and it wasn't long before I got curious about how they actually worked, what was happening under the hood to make them run. That curiosity turned into building my own 2D games, and I've been hooked on making things ever since.


I learn best by doing. Rather than just reading about a new concept, I like to dive in and build with it, and that hands-on approach taught me something just as valuable: breaking a large project down into smaller, manageable pieces. That habit is what lets me tackle complex features without getting overwhelmed by them.


Outside of the keyboard, you'll usually find me trying to get on a snowboard as often as I can, not the easiest hobby to keep up in a country without any mountains.
